What Is Breathe, Officially?

Officially, Breathe is categorized as a liquid dietary supplement delivered via dropper. The listed category keeps it distinct from a drug or medicine — it is designed to be one part of a broader wellness routine, not a standalone treatment.

Its formula is organized around four categories: natural extracts, respiratory support compounds, immunity boosters, and energy enhancers; the full breakdown is on the ingredients page. The brand's positioning centers on everyday respiratory wellness, a general wellness claim rather than a medical one.
