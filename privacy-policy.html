Privacy Policy

What this website collects, why, and what it does not do with it.

How SlimTide Pages Handle Your Information

This website provides information about SlimTide. It does not operate a checkout, process payments or maintain user accounts, and it does not ask for your name, address or payment details.

If you follow a link to the official seller, you leave this website. Anything you enter there — personal details, payment information, delivery address — is handled by that seller under its own privacy policy, which is the document governing your order. We never see, receive or store it.

Information SlimTide Pages May Collect Automatically

Like most websites, this one may record standard technical information when a page is requested: IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, pages viewed, time of visit and referring page.

This is used to keep the site working, understand which pages are read and identify technical problems. It is not used to build a profile of you as an individual, and it is not sold.

Cookies Used on SlimTide Pages

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. This site may use them for basic functionality and for aggregate analytics measuring which pages are visited.

Affiliate tracking cookies may also be set when you follow a link to the seller. These record that a visit originated here so any resulting commission is attributed correctly. They do not identify you personally to this website.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings without affecting your ability to read anything here.

Weight and Health Information on SlimTide Pages

Worth stating specifically, because weight is a sensitive subject.

We do not ask you to disclose any health information, operate no forms collecting weight, measurements, symptoms or medication details, and have no way to associate any health interest with you as an individual.

If you research weight or diet topics online generally, be aware that advertising networks on other sites may infer interests from browsing behaviour and target weight-loss advertising accordingly. That is a good reason to be thoughtful about where you enter health information anywhere on the web.

Third Parties Linked From SlimTide Pages

Outbound links point to the official seller and to a small number of social profiles. Those organisations operate their own privacy policies, and this one does not extend to them.

Your Rights Regarding SlimTide Site Data

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, to object to certain processing, or to complain to a supervisory authority.

Because this site collects very little and maintains no accounts, there is usually little to act on. Data relating to an actual order is held by the seller, and requests about it should be directed there.

Children and the SlimTide Website

This website is intended for adults. SlimTide is a dietary supplement for adults, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

SlimTide does not publish its ingredient amounts

All six ingredients are named, which is more than many products in this category manage. What is not published anywhere on the official source is how much of each one a capsule contains.

That gap matters, because effect depends on amount. Chromium has a defined daily requirement measured in micrograms. Garcinia trials used more than a gram of hydroxycitric acid daily. L-carnitine research typically used one to three grams. Without figures, none of it can be compared to what is actually in the bottle, so no milligram amounts appear anywhere on this website — inventing them would be worse than leaving them out.

Claims this SlimTide site does not repeat

The official source describes the formula as containing probiotics and supporting the growth of beneficial gut bacteria. No probiotic appears anywhere in its own published ingredient list, so that claim is absent here.

Its benefits section is also absent, because it describes a different product entirely — B-vitamins, cinnamon, bilberry, bacopa, Alpha-GPC and Himalayan salt, none of which is in SlimTide. Its ingredients introduction describes the formula as designed to support cognitive health in seniors, and one of its bonus descriptions names a completely different supplement. Recycled marketing copy of that kind is common across this category, and it is a reason to read any claim on a sales page carefully.

SlimTide contains caffeine, and two ingredients worth checking

Green tea extract supplies caffeine. The reference confirms this and advises anyone sensitive to stimulants to leave an hour between the capsule and their coffee. The amount is not published, so treat it as an unknown addition to your daily caffeine total and take the capsule in the morning.

Cayenne pepper extract commonly causes a burning sensation, heartburn or stomach upset, particularly on an empty stomach.

Chromium and alpha lipoic acid can both affect blood sugar, which matters a great deal if you take insulin or other diabetes medication — the combination can push glucose lower than intended. Alpha lipoic acid may also affect thyroid medication.

The reference's own advice is to consult a licensed doctor before taking any supplement, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication or managing a health condition. That is worth following rather than skimming.

If your relationship with food or your body feels difficult

Weight-management products sit close to a genuinely hard area of life. If thinking about food, weight or your body is causing you distress, or eating patterns feel out of your control, a supplement is not the right tool and this website is not the right resource. Speaking to a GP or a qualified professional is worth far more. In the US, the National Alliance for Eating Disorders helpline is available on 1-866-662-1235.
