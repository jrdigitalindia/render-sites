How to read the SlimTide compare-at prices

Each package is shown against a struck-through figure — $358, $537 and $1,074 — producing advertised savings of $200, $330 and $780.

Credit where it is due: unlike many pages in this category, these numbers are internally consistent. All three work out at exactly $179 per bottle, and the reference states that regular price directly.

What that figure represents is a different question. A reference price that does not appear to be the price anyone is actually charged is a framing device rather than a discount you are receiving. The comparison that holds up is between the packages themselves: $79, $69 and $49 per bottle.
