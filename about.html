About OptiPlax

A Simple Daily Habit for a Screen-Heavy World

OptiPlax exists for one reason: eye wellness is one of the easiest habits to overlook in a daily routine built around screens, commutes and long working hours. OptiPlax is a daily capsule designed to make that one habit a little easier to keep.

We built OptiPlax around a simple idea — a convenient, consistent daily serving that complements the habits that already matter: balanced meals, regular hydration, screen breaks and quality sleep. You can read more about the formula approach on our Formula & Ingredients page, or see how it fits into a daily schedule on our How It Works page.

OptiPlax is manufactured in an FDA-registered, GMP-certified facility in the USA. As with any dietary supplement, we encourage reviewing the current Supplement Facts panel on the product label and speaking with a healthcare professional if you take medication or manage a health condition.
