The three and six-bottle packages include all six digital guides, delivered as instant downloads. The two-bottle package includes none, which is stated clearly on the official pricing and repeated here so it does not come as a surprise at checkout.

How to Read the SlimTide Bonus Values

The $358 combined figure comes from the individual retail prices the seller attaches to each guide, and unlike some pages in this category the arithmetic adds up correctly.

What the figure represents is the question. These are digital guides given away with multi-bottle orders, and we could not find them sold separately at those prices anywhere. A stated retail value on a product only ever given away is promotional framing rather than an independent valuation — true across this industry rather than of this product specifically.

None of which makes the guides worthless. A useful guide is useful whatever number is printed beside it. It does mean the sensible approach is to judge the package on the supplement and the price, and treat the guides as a pleasant addition rather than as $358 of value you would otherwise have paid for.

One SlimTide bonus description worth noticing

The sixth guide's description on the official source refers to maximising the active ingredients in a completely different supplement, naming another product entirely.

It is a small thing and it does not affect what you receive. It is worth mentioning because it shows the bonus copy has been reused across unrelated funnels without being checked — which is a reasonable prompt to read every claim on a sales page with care rather than assuming it was written for the product in front of you.

A Note on the SlimTide Bonus Health Content

Several of these guides cover health topics — gut microbiota, skin, metabolic protocols. Treat any health guidance in a promotional e-book as general interest reading rather than personalised advice, particularly where it suggests supplements, fasting or restrictive eating patterns.

If your relationship with food or your body feels difficult

Weight-management products sit close to a genuinely hard area of life. If thinking about food, weight or your body is causing you distress, or eating patterns feel out of your control, a supplement is not the right tool and this website is not the right resource. Speaking to a GP or a qualified professional is worth far more. In the US, the National Alliance for Eating Disorders helpline is available on 1-866-662-1235.
