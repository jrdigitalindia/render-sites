The Formula

What is Gelatide?

Gelatide is a liquid dropper supplement in a 2 fl oz amber glass bottle, built around six named botanical extracts: green tea leaf, guarana seed, African mango seed, Coleus forskohlii root, capsicum annuum fruit and raspberry ketones.

It's a dietary supplement, not a medicine. Every botanical is named individually with its full Latin binomial rather than folded into a vague proprietary blend — you can read the complete breakdown on our Gelatide ingredients guide. No milligram amount is printed for any of the six, so the exact strength of each extract should be weighed with that in mind.

Gelatide is best suited to adults who find capsules hard to swallow, who tolerate caffeine reasonably well (two of the six botanicals are caffeine sources), and who want a short, fully-named ingredient list even without milligram figures. See our full official product overview for pricing and guarantee details.

The Mechanism

How Does Gelatide Work?

The label lists three target areas. Here's the mechanism behind each claim, in plain language.

Inside The Formula

Ingredients of Gelatide

Gelatide names six botanicals on its label, each with its Latin binomial. See the full ingredients breakdown below, or visit our complete ingredients guide for details and evidence levels.

Green Tea Leaf
Camellia sinensis

Moderate Evidence

The catechin and caffeine source in the formula. Well studied for metabolism generally, though research hasn't consistently moved appetite-related hormones specifically.

Guarana Seed
Paullinia cupana

Moderate Evidence

An Amazonian seed carrying two to four times the caffeine of coffee bean by weight, with its own fatigue-reduction research. Not a weight ingredient on its own.

African Mango Seed
Irvingia gabonensis

Weak Evidence

Carries much of the appetite-control marketing in this category, but its supporting trials were judged small, short and unconvincing on systematic review.

Coleus Forskohlii Root
Plectranthus barbatus

Mixed Evidence

The source of forskolin, which raises cyclic AMP in cells. Human trials show body-composition shifts without consistent overall weight change.

Capsicum Annuum Fruit
Capsicum annuum

Strongest Evidence

The capsaicinoid source, and the one ingredient here with a meta-analysis aimed directly at food intake, showing roughly 74 fewer kilocalories per meal.

Raspberry Ketones
Rubus idaeus

No Human Evidence

The aroma compound responsible for raspberry scent. A long-standing category inclusion, but no human trial of the isolated compound has been run.

No milligram amount is printed for any of the six botanicals. See our complete Gelatide ingredients guide for the full breakdown.

What is Gelatide used for?

Gelatide is a liquid dropper dietary supplement formulated to support healthy digestion, fat metabolism and natural appetite control, built around six named botanical extracts.

What are the main ingredients in Gelatide?

Green tea leaf, guarana seed, African mango seed, Coleus forskohlii root, capsicum annuum fruit and raspberry ketones. See our full ingredient breakdown for detail and evidence levels.

Does Gelatide contain caffeine?

Yes, from two of the six extracts: green tea leaf and guarana seed. The total caffeine amount per serving is not disclosed, so caffeine-sensitive individuals should take this into account.

How does the guarantee work?

60 days from the purchase date. The buyer is responsible for return shipping, and the refund is processed after the returned product is received.

Is Gelatide FDA approved?

No — no dietary supplement is FDA-approved the way a prescription drug is. Gelatide's three label claims are structure-function claims, a category the FDA permits without pre-approval provided they don't claim to treat a disease.

Who should speak with a doctor before trying Gelatide?

Anyone taking blood pressure, blood-thinning, thyroid or diabetes medication, anyone with a liver condition or caffeine sensitivity, and anyone pregnant or nursing should consult a healthcare professional first.
