Inside The Formula

Ingredients of NeuroSharp

NeuroSharp names six ingredients on its label. See the full ingredients breakdown below, or visit our complete ingredients guide for roles and details.

Ginkgo Biloba Leaf

Circulation & Antioxidant Support

Acts as an antioxidant and vasodilator, associated with improved cerebral blood flow, delivering more oxygen and nutrients to brain cells to support memory, focus and alertness.

St. John's Wort Extract

Mood & Emotional Balance

Known for mood-balancing effects, traditionally used to support emotional stability and clear thinking under pressure. Can interact with several common medications — see caution below.

N-Acetyl L-Carnitine HCl

Energy & Clarity

An amino acid derivative that supports mitochondrial energy production in neurons, associated with mental clarity and reduced brain fog.

Phosphatidylserine Complex

Memory & Neural Communication

An essential phospholipid in brain cell membranes, associated with healthy neural communication and memory formation.

Huperzine-A

Learning & Recall

Extracted from Chinese club moss, acts as a natural acetylcholinesterase inhibitor associated with increased acetylcholine levels and recall.

Bacopa Monnieri

Memory & Neuroprotection

An Ayurvedic herb associated with memory retention, learning speed and antioxidant, neuroprotective effects.

NeuroSharp — Frequently Asked Questions

What is NeuroSharp used for?

NeuroSharp is a dietary supplement formulated to support memory retention, focus, mental clarity and overall cognitive performance.

What are the main ingredients in NeuroSharp?

Ginkgo Biloba Leaf, St. John's Wort Extract, N-Acetyl L-Carnitine HCl, Phosphatidylserine Complex, Huperzine-A and Bacopa Monnieri. See our full ingredient breakdown for more detail.

How many capsules are in a bottle?

Each bottle contains 30 capsules, a 15-day supply at the suggested two-capsule daily serving.

How long before I notice results?

Some users notice changes within the first week, but the brand suggests daily use for at least 90 days, with the most significant results typically appearing after 6-8 weeks.

Does NeuroSharp contain stimulants?

No. NeuroSharp is formulated to be stimulant-free and non-habit-forming.

Does NeuroSharp interact with medication?

St. John's Wort, one of the six ingredients, is known to interact with a number of common medications, including antidepressants, blood thinners, and hormonal birth control. Speak with a healthcare professional before use if you take any prescription medication.

Is NeuroSharp suitable for everyone?

NeuroSharp is intended for adults 18 and older. It is not intended for anyone under 18, pregnant, or nursing without medical guidance.
