The OptiPlax Story

Built for a World That Rarely Looks Away From a Screen

OptiPlax was created with a simple observation: more of daily life happens on screens than ever before, yet eye wellness is often the last habit anyone thinks to build. OptiPlax was designed to make that habit easier — one capsule, one routine, one day at a time.

The goal was never to replace the fundamentals. Balanced meals, proper hydration, regular screen breaks and quality sleep still matter most. OptiPlax is meant to sit alongside those habits, not instead of them. For a closer look at putting that routine together, see building an eye wellness routine that sticks.
