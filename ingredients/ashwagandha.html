What Is Ashwagandha?

Ashwagandha is one of the most-studied adaptogens in Ayurvedic tradition, used for centuries to help the body adapt to physical and mental stress.

Its Role In The MaxForce Formula

It's the formula's dedicated stress-support ingredient, addressing a factor that indirectly affects energy, mood and physical performance.

Traditional Use & What The Research Says

A substantial body of research has examined Ashwagandha's relationship to stress response and hormone levels already within normal range.

Worth knowing: Its adaptogenic classification means it's studied for helping the body's overall stress response, not one narrow symptom.
