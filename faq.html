SlimTide contains caffeine, and two ingredients worth checking

Green tea extract supplies caffeine. The reference confirms this and advises anyone sensitive to stimulants to leave an hour between the capsule and their coffee. The amount is not published, so treat it as an unknown addition to your daily caffeine total and take the capsule in the morning.

Cayenne pepper extract commonly causes a burning sensation, heartburn or stomach upset, particularly on an empty stomach.

Chromium and alpha lipoic acid can both affect blood sugar, which matters a great deal if you take insulin or other diabetes medication — the combination can push glucose lower than intended. Alpha lipoic acid may also affect thyroid medication.

The reference's own advice is to consult a licensed doctor before taking any supplement, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication or managing a health condition. That is worth following rather than skimming.

If your relationship with food or your body feels difficult

Weight-management products sit close to a genuinely hard area of life. If thinking about food, weight or your body is causing you distress, or eating patterns feel out of your control, a supplement is not the right tool and this website is not the right resource. Speaking to a GP or a qualified professional is worth far more. In the US, the National Alliance for Eating Disorders helpline is available on 1-866-662-1235.
