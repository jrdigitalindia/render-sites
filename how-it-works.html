How It Works

Fitting OptiPlax Into a Normal Day

No complicated protocol — just a simple daily serving alongside the habits you already have.

1

Check the Current Label

Review the current OptiPlax Supplement Facts panel and follow the serving directions printed on the packaging.

2

Pick a Consistent Time

Pair your daily serving with an existing habit — breakfast, your morning coffee, or brushing your teeth — so it's easy to remember.

3

Pair It With Screen Breaks

Add simple visual breaks through the day, especially during long stretches of screen time.

4

Stay Consistent

Supplement habits work best as part of a routine. Give it time, and keep nutrition, hydration and sleep in the picture too.

A Few Habits Worth Pairing With It

  • Use the 20-20-20 approach during long screen sessions (every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds)
  • Keep a water bottle at your desk — hydration plays a real role in visual comfort
  • Adjust screen brightness and glare before reaching for anything else
  • Give yourself a proper wind-down before bed; sleep quality affects how your eyes feel the next day
