The SlimTide Ingredient Interaction Disclosure

This deserves its own section, because two ingredients in this formula share the same consideration and their effects can add together.

Chromium and alpha lipoic acid can both lower blood glucose. For anyone taking insulin or other diabetes medication this is a genuine interaction rather than a theoretical one, and the presence of both in a single capsule makes a medical check worth doing properly. Garcinia may also affect blood sugar.

Alpha lipoic acid and L-carnitine may both affect thyroid medication. Anyone taking levothyroxine should mention the supplement to their doctor.

Green tea extract supplies caffeine in an amount the seller does not publish, and carries considerations around iron absorption, anticoagulant medication and, at high concentrated doses, liver health.

Cayenne pepper extract can cause burning, heartburn or stomach upset, particularly on an empty stomach, and may aggravate reflux, gastritis, ulcers or IBS.

Garcinia cambogia has case reports associating high-dose products with liver injury, and may interact with serotonergic medication such as SSRIs.

The SlimTide Formula Disclosure

Six ingredients are named throughout this website: cayenne pepper extract, alpha lipoic acid, green tea leaf extract, garcinia cambogia extract, L-carnitine and chromium. This is the ingredient list published by the official source.

Individual ingredient amounts are not published anywhere on that source. No milligram or microgram figures appear anywhere on this website. Inventing them would be misleading, and it would also be pointless, since the whole value of a published amount is that it can be checked.

One consequence worth stating: because amounts are unknown, no ingredient in this formula can be compared to the doses used in published research. If you encounter a page quoting precise amounts for this product, that information is not coming from the seller.

The capsule shell material is also not stated. The seller's own FAQ advises checking the label to determine whether it is gelatin or plant-based HPMC, which matters for anyone avoiding animal products.

The SlimTide Research Disclosure

Research referenced on this website concerns individual ingredients, studied on their own, at doses set by researchers, usually in specific populations.

That research does not demonstrate that the finished SlimTide product produces the same outcomes. No clinical trial of this finished formula has been published as far as this website is aware, and nothing here should be read as product-level clinical evidence.

The evidence quality also varies substantially across the six ingredients, from chromium's established status as an essential nutrient to garcinia cambogia's unfavourable systematic reviews. The individual ingredient pages characterise each one honestly rather than presenting them as equally supported.

SlimTide contains caffeine, and two ingredients worth checking

If your relationship with food or your body feels difficult

Weight-management products sit close to a genuinely hard area of life. If thinking about food, weight or your body is causing you distress, or eating patterns feel out of your control, a supplement is not the right tool and this website is not the right resource. Speaking to a GP or a qualified professional is worth far more. In the US, the National Alliance for Eating Disorders helpline is available on 1-866-662-1235.
