Does Gelatide Contain Caffeine? What You Need to Know

Published November 7, 2026 · 6 min read

It's a fair question to ask before adding any new supplement to your routine, and the answer for Gelatide is yes — with an important caveat worth understanding before you order.

Where the Caffeine Comes From

Two of Gelatide's six named botanicals are caffeine sources. Green tea leaf (Camellia sinensis) contains both catechins and caffeine, and guarana seed (Paullinia cupana) carries two to four times the caffeine of coffee bean by weight. Together, these two ingredients give Gelatide a genuine caffeine content — see our full ingredient breakdown for what each one is individually studied at.

What Isn't Disclosed

Here's the caveat: the total caffeine amount per 2mL serving is not printed anywhere on the Gelatide label. Neither is a milligram figure for green tea leaf or guarana seed individually. That means anyone sensitive to caffeine has to gauge their own tolerance by how they feel, rather than by a number they can check in advance or compare to, say, a cup of coffee.

Why This Matters More for Some People Than Others

For most healthy adults who already drink coffee or tea regularly, an undisclosed-but-present caffeine content from two plant sources is a manageable unknown. For a few groups, it's worth extra caution:

  • Anyone who identifies as caffeine-sensitive or avoids caffeine for sleep or anxiety reasons
  • Anyone taking medication for blood pressure, thyroid function, or anxiety, where caffeine can interact
  • Anyone pregnant or nursing, where caffeine intake is typically capped at a known daily limit
  • Anyone already consuming caffeine from coffee, tea, or other supplements earlier in the day

A Practical Way to Approach It

Since the amount isn't disclosed, a cautious approach is to try Gelatide on its own first — without stacking it on top of your usual coffee or tea — and see how you feel before combining it with other caffeine sources. Taking it in the morning, as suggested, also gives any caffeine content the most time to clear your system before bedtime. See our how Gelatide works guide for the full suggested routine.

Caffeine and the Rest of the Formula

Worth noting: the caffeine sources aren't incidental to Gelatide's claims. Green tea's catechins and guarana's stimulant effect both relate to the "fat metabolism" claim on the label, alongside Coleus forskohlii's separate, non-caffeine mechanism. So the caffeine content isn't just a side effect to tolerate — it's part of why these two botanicals were included in the first place.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much caffeine is in one Gelatide serving?

The exact amount is not disclosed on the label. It comes from two sources — green tea leaf and guarana seed — so caffeine-sensitive individuals should take this into account and monitor how they feel.

Can I take Gelatide alongside my morning coffee?

That's a personal tolerance question best discussed with a healthcare professional, especially since the combined caffeine total from coffee plus Gelatide isn't something you can calculate precisely.

Is Gelatide caffeine-free?

No. Two of its six botanicals are caffeine sources, even though the overall amount per serving isn't printed on the label.
