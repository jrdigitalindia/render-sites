How These SlimTide Articles Are Written

Five of the eight cover foundations — weight management, metabolism, appetite, daily habits and exercise — and each concludes that these matter more than any supplement. That is not false modesty; it is what the evidence supports.

The other three cover ingredients in the formula, and they are candid about where the evidence is strong, where it is thin, and where the measured effects are real but far smaller than marketing implies.

How to read the research on this SlimTide page

The studies described here examined individual ingredients, usually on their own, at doses set by the researchers. That research provides scientific context for why an ingredient appears in a formula.

It does not demonstrate that the finished SlimTide product produces the same outcomes. No clinical trial of this finished formula has been published, so nothing here is product-level clinical evidence.
